01 / Studio

Small by design. Serious by default.

Redon Games is an independent studio building original worlds, useful technology, and a disciplined way for small teams to make both.

The work behind the work.

We are early, independent, and deliberate about our scope. That gives us room to keep creative intent close to implementation instead of treating it as a document that gets lost along the way.

  • Make fewer things, with clearer reasons.
  • Invite feedback before polish makes change expensive.
  • Treat technology as a studio craft.

Practice

Close context

People making the decision should stay near the work that carries it out.

Focus

Original worlds

Our games begin with a point of view, then earn their detail through play.

Approach

Useful systems

Tools should make a team more legible to itself, not more dependent on mystery.
