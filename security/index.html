SPS Desktop / private beta

Security is part of the work.

A small beta still needs a clear way to report a problem. Please help us improve SPS without putting people or systems at risk.

Last updated: July 28, 2026

Report a vulnerability

Email redongames1234@gmail.com with the subject “SPS Security.” Include a clear description, affected SPS version, steps to reproduce, impact, and any safe proof of concept. Do not include real secrets, private project files, or another person's data.

Test safely

Only test on systems and accounts you own or have written permission to test. Do not disrupt service, use denial-of-service techniques, attempt social engineering, access another person's workspace, or probe third-party services not operated by Redon Games.

What we are securing

The SPS beta includes a local-first Desktop app, owner-hosted project workspaces, invited member connections, authentication, and optional plugins or external tools. The product uses signed node requests and local connection controls, but beta software can still contain defects. Keep the app current and keep backups of important work.

What happens next

We will acknowledge a good-faith report, assess the issue, and coordinate a fix or mitigation when practical. There is no bug bounty program during this private beta. Please give us reasonable time to investigate before sharing details publicly.
